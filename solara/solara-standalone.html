The Origin

From Milan, to the World

Where the craft of luxury eyewear was reinvented for a new generation.

In 2018, three friends — a materials scientist, a fashion editor, and a master optician — sat in a café in Milan arguing about why truly exceptional sunglasses didn't exist at a price that didn't feel like extortion.

They spent two years visiting artisan workshops across the Veneto region, sourcing the finest bio-acetate sheets, commissioning custom titanium alloys, and working with optical labs to develop proprietary polarized lens technology.

SOLARA launched with six styles. They sold out in eleven days.

Six years later, we're still driven by the same argument in that café: that design, materials, and optics don't have to be at war with each other — or with your conscience.

FAQ

How long does shipping take?

Standard 3–5 business days. Express 1–2 days. Free standard shipping on orders over $300 worldwide.

What's your return policy?

30-day hassle-free returns on unworn pairs in original packaging.

Does the warranty cover scratches?

Lifetime warranty covers manufacturing defects. Lens scratches from normal wear not covered, but replacement lenses available at cost.

Do you offer prescription lenses?

Yes! Send your prescription after purchase and our optical partners will fit it into any SOLARA frame.
