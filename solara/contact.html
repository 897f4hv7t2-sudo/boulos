Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping is 3–5 business days. Express 1–2 days. Free standard shipping on orders over $300. We ship worldwide.

What's your return policy?

30-day hassle-free returns on unworn pairs in original packaging. Start a return directly from your order confirmation email.

Does the warranty cover scratches?

Our lifetime warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures. Lens scratches from normal wear are not covered, but we offer replacement lenses at cost.

Do you offer prescription lenses?

Yes! Send us your prescription after purchase and our optical partners will fit prescription lenses into any SOLARA frame. Additional fees apply.
